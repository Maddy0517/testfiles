# Indian Alcohol Industry Stock Recommendations - December 2024

## Executive Summary
The Indian alcohol industry presents significant growth opportunities with the market projected to reach $61.35 billion by FY 2025-26. Premium segments are expected to grow 10-25% YoY, driven by a burgeoning middle class and evolving consumer preferences.
